# ARGUS Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: The Hook
**The Salesloft Breach.** 700 companies exposed. How? One unguarded door.
Agents are being given the keys to the kingdom, but no one is watching what they actually do with them.

## Slide 2: The Problem
**The Action Layer is Unguarded.**
We spend millions securing the *input* (firewalls, prompt filters) and the *data* (encryption). But when the AI agent decides to *act*, it operates with full permissions.

## Slide 3: The Demo
**Crime Scene & Solution**
*(Live Demo: Show Prompt Injection succeeding in Unprotected Agent, then quarantined by ARGUS)*

## Slide 4: The Solution
**3-Layer Defense**
1. **Intent Manifest:** Locks the agent into a specific, mathematically bounded scope.
2. **Lobster Trap:** Veea's proxy engine catches every action.
3. **Gemini Pro:** Translates security jargon into plain English for human reviewers.

## Slide 5: Differentiation
**Input vs. Action**
Everyone else filters the prompt. We evaluate the *action*. 
If an injection slips through, ARGUS catches the resulting unauthorized behavior.

## Slide 6: The Market
**78.1% of enterprise AI has zero action-level security.**
Average breach cost: $4.4M. ARGUS provides the necessary audit trail for SOC2 and HIPAA compliance.
